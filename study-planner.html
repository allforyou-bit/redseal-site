How the plan works

The planner scales a proven four-phase method to the time you have. Each phase targets a different weakness so you are not just re-reading what you already know:

  • Baseline — one full mock exam cold, then broad practice to find your weak topics.
  • Topic rotation — attack your two weakest blocks, one per session.
  • Wrong bank + hard mode — clear your Mistakes tab, then hard questions on strong topics.
  • Mock exams — timed, full-length runs reviewing every wrong answer between attempts.

Pass mark is 70% for every Red Seal trade. The daily question target is set so you cover your trade's material at least twice before exam day. See the full method in our study guide.

Frequently asked questions

How long should I study for the Red Seal exam?

Most working apprentices give themselves 4–8 weeks of consistent practice — about 45–60 minutes on weekdays and a bit more on weekends. The planner scales the four-phase schedule to whatever time you have.

How many practice questions per day?

A sustainable target is 20–30 questions per weekday, reading every explanation. The planner calculates a target from your exam date and your trade's bank so you cover the material at least twice.

What if my exam is very soon?

If you have under two weeks, the planner compresses to essentials: daily mock exams and clearing your wrong-answer bank. It will tell you honestly if the timeline is tight.
