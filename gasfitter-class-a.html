About the Red Seal Gasfitter — Class A Exam

Where this applies: Gasfitter — Class A carries a Red Seal endorsement in most provinces (examined through jurisdictions such as BC, Alberta and Manitoba). In Ontario and Saskatchewan the Red Seal gasfitter endorsement is not recognized: Ontario licenses gas work through TSSA (Gas Technician G3/G2/G1) and Saskatchewan through TSASK/SATCC (General/Domestic Gas Fitter). This question bank covers the Red Seal (CSA B149.1-based) scope, which overlaps heavily with the TSSA G2/G1 and TSASK material.

The Red Seal Gasfitter (Class A) exam is the interprovincial certification for gas fitters working on all types and pressures of natural gas and propane systems. Class A certification covers residential, commercial, and industrial gas installations including high-pressure supply, appliance commissioning, venting design, and combustion analysis.

Red Seal does not currently publish a question count for this exam — new exams built on the updated occupational standard are under development. It is multiple choice, four options with one correct answer, based on the Red Seal Occupational Standard (RSOS) and aligned with CSA B149.1 Natural Gas and Propane Installation Code, with a passing score of 70%. Exam time is 4 hours.

  • Appliances & Combustion Equipment — Furnaces, boilers, water heaters, unit heaters, infrared heaters. Burner adjustment, heat exchanger inspection, ignition systems, and commissioning.
  • Gas Supply Systems — Meter sets, regulators (service, line, appliance), pressure testing, odourant, and first/second stage regulation.
  • Piping Systems — Pipe sizing using pressure drop tables, pipe materials (black steel, CSST, copper, PE), fittings, supports, and leak detection.
  • Venting Systems — Category I–IV venting, natural/induced/direct vent, common vent sizing, termination clearances.
  • Combustion Theory & Air — Stoichiometric combustion, excess air, CO/CO₂ analysis, combustion air requirements (sized on input per CSA B149.1, about 1 in² per 1,000 BTU/h).
  • Safety, Codes & Regulations — CSA B149.1, provincial requirements, permits, pressure testing, gas leak response, purging.

What Red Seal publishes for Gasfitter Class A

Unlike the other trades on this site, Red Seal does not currently publish a question count or block breakdown for the Gasfitter (Class A) exam — the trade’s exams are being rewritten to an updated occupational standard. Study the whole standard rather than chasing a weighting, and check the trade page at red-seal.ca before you book.

Every question is four-option multiple choice with exactly one correct answer, and the pass mark is 70%. Expect an applied exam: roughly 50–60% of questions are procedural and application style and another 25–35% are critical thinking, with only 10–20% straight knowledge and recall. Controls, installation, commissioning and appliance service together account for almost three-quarters of the paper, so hands-on installation and service judgement matters more than memorized definitions.
