Red Seal Completion Bonus: the $5,000, checked against canada.ca

Who qualifies, when to apply, which documents your province or territory asks for, and what the official pages do not say.

Updated November 2026 Applications open November 16, 2026
Status on November 2, 2026: applications are not open yet. Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) says the application portal opens on November 16, 2026. Apply only through the Government of Canada, starting from the official Red Seal Completion Bonus page on canada.ca. This page is an independent guide, not a government site.

The short answer

What it isA $5,000 federal payment for journeypersons who complete an apprenticeship and get certified in a designated Red Seal trade. It is part of the federal Team Canada Strong initiative.
How much$5,000 for each designated Red Seal trade in which you completed an apprenticeship and were certified on or after April 1, 2025.
Who can applyCanadian citizens, permanent residents and protected persons who completed an apprenticeship program as a registered apprentice in a designated Red Seal trade, are certified journeypersons, and are not high school students.
Applications openNovember 16, 2026. The official pages give no end date for the application period.
DeadlineCertified April 1, 2025 to November 15, 2026: apply by May 31, 2027. Certified on or after November 16, 2026: apply within 12 months of your certification date.
DocumentsSubmit every supporting document within 28 calendar days of your application date. If they arrive late, you have to submit a new application.
HelpProgram line 1-866-742-3644 (toll-free), 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday to Friday. TTY 1-866-909-9757, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday to Friday. Not on statutory holidays. In person at any Service Canada Centre. (Contact us)

A designated Red Seal trade is one your province or territory of registration designates and the Red Seal Program recognizes. Red Seal publishes the list of Red Seal trades.

What the official pages say, and what they do not

News coverage in October 2026 repeated details that the canada.ca program pages do not support. Each row below was checked against those pages on November 2, 2026.

QuestionWhat the official pages say
Is it a one-time payment?Not once per person. ESDC says journeypersons can receive the bonus for each designated Red Seal trade in which they completed an apprenticeship and received certification, so two qualifying trades mean two payments.
Is there a $50,000 maximum?The program pages state no maximum. Some October 2026 articles give a $50,000 cap; the official pages checked on November 2, 2026 do not.
Are applications open now?No. They open on November 16, 2026.
Can a trade qualifier (challenger) get it?The eligibility list requires that you completed an apprenticeship program as a registered apprentice in a designated Red Seal trade. The pages do not mention trade qualifiers. If you were certified without a registered apprenticeship, call the program line before counting on the bonus.
Do I need the Red Seal endorsement, or is my provincial certificate enough?The pages use both “Red Seal certification” and “certification in a designated Red Seal trade”, and the document list asks for the certificates your province or territory issued, such as an Alberta Journeyperson Certificate. They do not say whether a certificate without the Red Seal endorsement counts. In Red Seal trades, most provinces and territories use the Red Seal exam as the final certification exam, so most apprentices earn both at once. In Alberta the Red Seal exam is an option, so if your certificate has no Red Seal, call the program line before counting on the bonus.
Is it the old Apprenticeship Completion Grant?No. The $2,000 Apprenticeship Completion Grant ended on March 31, 2025 and no longer accepts applications (canada.ca). The completion bonus is a separate program.
Is it taxable?The program pages do not say. The old Apprenticeship Completion Grant was described as a taxable cash grant (canada.ca), but nothing on the new pages says the same rule applies to the bonus.
Is there an application fee?The official pages mention none.

Check your deadline

The rules behind the checker, from ESDC’s Do you qualify page:

  • Certified before April 1, 2025: outside the window the official pages describe.
  • Certified April 1, 2025 to November 15, 2026: apply from November 16, 2026, no later than May 31, 2027.
  • Certified on or after November 16, 2026: apply within 12 months of your certification date.
  • Every case: upload all supporting documents within 28 calendar days of your application date, or start a new application. ESDC says applications are considered only if submitted within these deadlines.

What to have ready before November 16

Personal information

  • Your Social Insurance Number (SIN), found on your SIN card, SIN confirmation letter or income tax documents
  • Your mailing address, and an email address (optional, but ESDC recommends one)
  • Banking details for direct deposit: financial institution number, branch (transit) number and account number. Service Canada recommends direct deposit as the fastest way to be paid.

Apprenticeship information

  • Your certification date
  • Your province or territory of certification
  • Your apprentice identification number
  • The Red Seal trade name

Have electronic copies ready before you start: you upload them as part of the application. ESDC lists these proofs of apprenticeship completion and certification, and says the list may be updated.

Permanent residents and protected persons

A permanent resident whose SIN does not start with 9 needs no proof of status. A permanent resident whose SIN starts with 9 needs one of: a permanent resident card; a Confirmation of Permanent Residence (IMM 5292, IMM 5688 or IMM 5509) that is not expired; a Verification of Status (IMM 5716); a Verification of Landing (IMM 5617); a Record of Landing (IMM 1000); or a Notice of Decision from the Immigration and Refugee Board. A protected person whose SIN starts with 9 needs a Confirmation of Permanent Residence that is not expired, a Verification of Status (IMM 5716) or an Immigration and Refugee Board Notice of Decision. Full list: ESDC’s Prepare to apply page.

Not certified yet? The bonus starts at certification

The bonus goes to journeypersons: in ESDC’s words, tradespeople who have met the certification requirements for their trade and received a Certificate of Qualification or an equivalent credential from a provincial or territorial apprenticeship authority. For a registered apprentice, the certification exam is usually the last of those requirements. In Red Seal trades, most provinces and territories use the Red Seal exam as the final certification exam; in Alberta it is an option after the requirements for Alberta journeyperson certification are complete (see Red Seal vs provincial certification).

If you certify on or after November 16, 2026, your deadline is 12 months from your certification date, and the official pages give no end date for the program. There is no reason to rush into the exam to catch the bonus, but there is nothing to claim until you are certified.

Every Red Seal exam is multiple choice with four options, and the pass mark is 70% in every trade. For question counts, time limits and how to book by province or territory, see the Red Seal exam format guide and the exam registration guide.

Other Team Canada Strong supports

ESDC’s Team Canada Strong backgrounder (October 23, 2026) lists these alongside the completion bonus:

  • Apprenticeship Training Grant: a $400 weekly top-up while apprentices attend mandatory in-class technical training, paid in addition to Employment Insurance benefits. It applies to eligible weeks of technical training beginning on or after January 3, 2027. The backgrounder does not say how to apply or give a total amount.
  • Apprenticeship Continuation Support: income support for periods of unemployment between in-class training and work. No amount is given.
  • Build Canada Apprenticeship Service (employers): up to $10,000 for small and medium-sized employers that hire a first-year apprentice in an eligible Red Seal trade.
  • Red Seal Program changes: digitizing the program with online exams, digital logbooks and secure credentials, and a single national registered apprenticeship number. No dates are given.

ESDC describes Team Canada Strong as an $8 billion investment to recruit, train and hire 80,000 to 100,000 new Red Seal skilled trades workers by 2030–2031. For the trades with the strongest projected demand, see skilled trades in demand in Canada in 2026.

For how Employment Insurance works today for apprentices referred to full-time technical training, from the 16-digit reference code to the waiting week, see EI for apprentices in technical training.

Already working in your trade? The tradesperson’s tools deduction lets employed tradespeople deduct the cost of tools they have to provide above $1,471, up to $1,000 for 2025, and eligible apprentice mechanics can claim more.

Still in technical training? The Canada Apprentice Loan lends up to $4,000 per period of technical training, interest-free, for up to 5 periods.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Red Seal Completion Bonus?

$5,000 for each designated Red Seal trade in which you completed an apprenticeship and were certified on or after April 1, 2025. The canada.ca program pages checked on November 2, 2026 state no maximum amount.

When can I apply for the Red Seal Completion Bonus?

Applications open on November 16, 2026, when Employment and Social Development Canada opens the application portal. They were not open as of November 2, 2026.

Who qualifies for the Red Seal Completion Bonus?

Canadian citizens, permanent residents and protected persons who completed an apprenticeship program as a registered apprentice in a designated Red Seal trade, are certified journeypersons in that trade, were certified on or after April 1, 2025, and are not high school students. Adult learning and professional upgrading classes do not count as high school.

What is the deadline to apply for the Red Seal Completion Bonus?

If your certification date is between April 1, 2025 and November 15, 2026, you must apply by May 31, 2027. If it is on or after November 16, 2026, you must apply within 12 months of your certification date. All supporting documents are due within 28 calendar days of your application date, or you have to submit a new application.

Can a trade qualifier or challenger get the Red Seal Completion Bonus?

The official eligibility list requires that you completed an apprenticeship program as a registered apprentice in a designated Red Seal trade, and the pages do not mention trade qualifiers. If you were certified without a registered apprenticeship, call the program line (1-866-742-3644) before counting on the bonus.

What documents do I need for the Red Seal Completion Bonus?

Your Social Insurance Number, mailing address, direct deposit banking details, certification date, province or territory of certification, apprentice identification number and Red Seal trade name, plus electronic copies of the certificates your province or territory issued, such as a Journeyperson Certificate in Alberta or a Certificate of Qualification and Certificate of Apprenticeship in Ontario. Permanent residents whose SIN begins with 9 and protected persons also need proof of status in Canada.

Is the Red Seal Completion Bonus the same as the Apprenticeship Completion Grant?

No. The $2,000 Apprenticeship Completion Grant ended on March 31, 2025 and no longer accepts applications. The Red Seal Completion Bonus is a separate program under Team Canada Strong.
