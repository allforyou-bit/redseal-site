About the 309A Red Seal Exam — Construction Electrician

The 309A Construction Electrician Red Seal exam is Canada's national certification for electricians working on residential, commercial, and light industrial construction projects. A 309A Red Seal endorsement proves your competency against the Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) and RSOS standards, allowing you to work anywhere in Canada without provincial re-examination.

The exam consists of 100 multiple-choice questions drawn from the 2023 Red Seal Occupational Standard, requiring a minimum score of 70%. Exam duration is up to 4 hours.

Official 309A Exam Breakdown (red-seal.ca)

Red Seal publishes how the 100 questions on the real 309A exam are distributed across its Major Work Activities:

  • A — Performs common occupational skills — 11 questions (11%)
  • B — Installs, services and maintains generating, distribution and service systems — 28 questions (28%)
  • C — Installs, services and maintains wiring systems — 30 questions (30%)
  • D — Installs, services and maintains motors and control systems — 21 questions (21%)
  • E — Installs, services and maintains signalling and communication systems — 10 questions (10%)

Every question is four-option multiple choice with one correct answer, the writing time is four hours and the pass mark is 70%. Source: 309A exam information, red-seal.ca. Exams are periodically rewritten to updated occupational standards, so check that page before you book.

What this question bank covers

These are the topic areas in this practice bank and what each one covers. The real 309A exam has 100 questions and a four-hour writing time. How those questions are distributed across the official blocks is published by Red Seal — see the 309A exam information page.

  • Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) — Rules & Calculations — The most important block. Box fill calculations (Rule 12-3034), conductor ampacity (Table 2), conduit fill, service entrance sizing, load calculations for dwellings. You must know CEC Part I cold.
  • Wiring Methods & Materials — NMD90, AC90, TECK90, EMT, rigid conduit, cable tray, underground wiring, and their Code-permitted applications. Installation requirements and protection rules.
  • Motors & Motor Controls — Motor starting methods (DOL, star-delta, soft-starter, VFD), overload protection selection, contactor sizing, and motor nameplate data interpretation.
  • Electrical Theory — Ohm's Law, power factor, transformer ratios, three-phase calculations, impedance in AC circuits. Expect calculation questions.
  • Safety Systems & GFCI/AFCI — Ground fault protection requirements, arc fault circuit interrupters, bonding and grounding (Section 10), equipotential bonding for pools and spas.
  • Special Occupancies & Systems — Hazardous locations (Section 18), emergency systems, exit lighting, HVAC disconnect requirements.

Exam Strategy for 309A

Know your Code tables. Multiple exam questions require table lookups — ampacity tables, conduit fill charts, demand factor tables. The 309A Red Seal exam is closed-book — the CEC is not permitted — so you must know these tables and how to apply them from memory.

Master box fill calculations. These appear consistently on every 309A exam. Know the cubic-inch volumes per conductor size, device, fitting, and clamp from memory.

Three-phase calculations. Know the formulas for balanced 3-phase loads (P = √3 × V × I × PF), transformer kVA sizing, and line vs. phase voltage relationships.
