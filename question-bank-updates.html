Question Bank Updates

A dated record of what was corrected in the practice question banks, in the PDFs made from them, and in the exam facts stated on this site.

Updated October 2026

How corrections are made

In September 2026 all eleven question banks were audited. Each finding was put to a reviewer whose job was to defend the question, and most of the findings that survived were checked again by a second, independent reviewer.

  • Wrong or disputable answer: the question is removed. It is not edited until it passes. Removed questions leave the free quiz, the free pages and the PDFs.
  • Wrong explanation: the explanation is corrected. The corrected line is written from a source the corrector opened and is checked by an independent verifier before it goes live. The question, its options and its answer stay the same.
  • New questions go live only after a break attempt and a source check. They replace a question that tested the same fact as another, or bring a bank back to its official exam length. A reviewer tries to break each one and its source is checked independently; candidates that fail are dropped, not revised.
  • Findings that are not defects are dismissed. Those questions are left as they were.

Later corrections are added to this page with their date. To report an error, use the Contact page.

Exam facts and site pages

  • 2026-10-05 309A pages (quiz page, exam tips, common mistakes and career guide) and their search markup, checked against Electrical Safety Authority bulletins on the 2024 Ontario Electrical Safety Code, Alberta STANDATA 24-ECI-012 and the CSA index to the 2021 Canadian Electrical Code: GFCI protection of receptacles is Rule 26-704 (5-15R and 5-20R receptacles within 1.5 m of sinks, bathtubs or shower stalls, and outdoors within 2.5 m of finished grade), not Rule 26-712, and garages and unfinished basements were taken off that list. AFCI protection is Rule 26-658, not 26-724. Rule 8-104 is maximum circuit loading, not service conductor sizing. Ampacity correction factors are Tables 5A to 5D under Rule 4-004, not Rule 14-012 or Table D8. Motor branch-circuit overcurrent protection now points to Rule 28-200 and Table 29, not Table 28, in place of a 250% figure for time-delay fuses, which was wrong. Box fill under Rule 12-3034 is described as a conductor count from Table 23 with deductions, not as cubic-inch volumes. Statements that particular rules or topics appear on every exam were removed.
  • 2026-10-05 306A pages, checked against the National Plumbing Code of Canada 2020 and red-seal.ca: the minimum trap seal depth is 38 mm, or 50 mm on fixtures draining to an acid waste system, and the code sets no maximum (the quiz page's search markup and the exam-tips guide had said 50 mm to 100 mm). Maximum trap arm lengths now follow Table 2.5.6.3, for example 2.4 m for NPS 2 and 3.6 m for NPS 3, where two guides had said 1.5 m and 1.8 m. Venting rules are in Division B, Section 2.5, not Part 7, and a fixture-clearance table number that does not exist in the code was removed. On the quiz page, the answer on exam topics now lists Red Seal's seven Major Work Activities with their question counts; the page no longer says the practice bank covers every official block or that every explanation cites an NPC section; and the four-hour time limit and 70% pass mark are credited to SkilledTradesBC, which publishes them, instead of red-seal.ca.
  • 2026-10-05 Apprenticeship length on the 309A and 306A career guides and in their search markup: totals said to apply in most provinces (5 periods, about 9,000 hours) contradicted other figures on the same pages. They were replaced with the hours published for each trade by Skilled Trades Ontario, SkilledTradesBC and Alberta Tradesecrets.
  • 2026-10-01 Information pages: an unsourced figure for how many Canadians hold a Red Seal endorsement was replaced with the Statistics Canada count of 2024 trade certificates issued with a Red Seal endorsement. 420B is named Ironworker (Generalist) throughout; Structural/Ornamental Ironworker (420A) and Reinforcing Rodworker (452A) are separate Red Seal trades. The number of Red Seal trades is given as 54, the red-seal.ca list as of October 2026, instead of “over 50”.
  • 2026-10-01 llms.txt, the summary file for AI assistants, now says what the 313A and 420B pages already said: British Columbia already uses the new Red Seal exams for these two trades, while red-seal.ca still lists them as under development.
  • 2026-09-30 Wages on 14 salary and career pages and 2 general guides: ranges without a source were replaced with Government of Canada Job Bank figures (low, median and high wage by occupation code, region and reference year), or rewritten without a number where no public source exists.
  • 2026-09-30 Search markup on 8 common-mistakes pages: 24 question-and-answer entries had been cut off mid-sentence. They were rebuilt in full from each page's visible text.
  • 2026-09-30 Exam conditions: pages no longer call the exams “closed-book”. You cannot bring your own notes or reference books, but the reference material an exam needs, such as a formula sheet or acronym list, is supplied.
  • 2026-09-30 Mock exam descriptions on several pages said the practice mocks follow the official exam proportions. They now say the mocks draw from every topic in the practice bank. Statements about how candidates do on the exam, which no published data supports, were removed from guide pages, and four page descriptions that had been cut off mid-sentence were completed.
  • 2026-09-29 Exam specifications, fees and wages were re-checked against primary sources (red-seal.ca and provincial exam pages, provincial fee schedules and Government of Canada Job Bank): 1,338 stated facts checked, 660 corrections on 82 pages. They include the edition of the Red Seal Occupational Standard named for each trade, the new 313A exam on the 2024 standard, official Major Work Activity counts in place of earlier splits that were not Red Seal's (420B and 421A), exam fees in four provinces, and wage figures that now give their Job Bank occupation code, range, median and data year.
  • 2026-09-29 Topic areas: wording on several pages and on the PDF covers presented the banks' topic areas as set by the official exam weightings. They are not. The topic areas are this site's own study groupings, not one-to-one with the official Major Work Activities, which each trade's quiz page lists from red-seal.ca. The wording was removed.
  • 2026-09-22 Three exam statistics that cannot be derived from Red Seal's published data were removed or rewritten (Gasfitter, 420B and 442A guides). Four study-tip boxes whose headings suggested exam-result data, which Red Seal does not publish, were renamed.
  • 2026-09-15 The exam format guide's search markup gave the wrong exam length for three trades, and three guides gave a wrong time per question. Both were corrected.
  • 2026-09-14 The trades guide still showed the question counts from before the audit on all eleven trade cards; they were restated. Three exam-tip guides gave an exam length that contradicted the same page, and were corrected.
