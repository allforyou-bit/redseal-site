Free Red Seal Trade Simulators

Run a system, take readings, then find the fault. Each simulator works in the browser on a phone or a computer, with no sign-up.

Updated November 2026 4 simulators Free, no sign-up

Each simulator is a simplified teaching model of one system. It is not a real machine and not a service or repair procedure, so the readings are relative or example values. Use it to see what a test should show, then practise exam questions for your trade.

The simulators

421A Closed-Loop Hydrostatic Drive Simulator Run a generic closed-loop hydrostatic drive in neutral, forward, reverse and stall, then find 5 drive faults from relative gauge and flow readings at the test ports. What each one covers

Hydrostatic drive simulator

Modes: Explore, Drive, Find the fault

  • What each line and valve in the drive does
  • Drive operation in neutral, forward and reverse, and charge pump circuits
  • Finding a drive fault from loop, charge and servo pressures, and from charge pump and motor case drain flow

DC circuit fault simulator

Modes: Explore, Meter, Find the fault

  • Voltage and resistance readings between test points
  • Voltage-drop testing across a switch, a connector and the battery cables
  • Telling an open from a short to ground or a high-resistance connection

Motor starter control circuit simulator

Modes: Explore, Operate and meter, Find the fault

  • Start, stop and seal-in in a three-wire control circuit, and why the motor stays stopped when the supply comes back
  • Voltage and resistance readings across each part, with START held or released
  • Finding an open fuse, overload contact, pushbutton, seal-in contact or coil, and a START contact that stays closed

Planetary gear set simulator

Modes: Explore, Operate, Find the fault

  • The six ways to drive one member and hold another, direct drive and neutral, with the ratio and torque of each
  • Why the held member carries a reaction torque, and why a weakened band or clutch slips under a heavy load before a light one
  • Finding a worn, leaking or unapplied band or clutch, or low line pressure, from speed and pressure tests

How to get the most from them

  1. Explore first. Tap each part of the drawing to read what it does before you change anything.
  2. Take readings on the healthy system. Knowing the normal reading is what makes a wrong one stand out.
  3. Then pick a fault. Read the symptom, take the readings, and choose a cause. If the readings do not point to one cause yet, take more before you choose.
  4. Finish with questions. Each simulator links to free practice questions on the same topic for your trade.
