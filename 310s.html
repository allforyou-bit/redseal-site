About the 310S Red Seal Exam — Automotive Service Technician

The 310S Automotive Service Technician Red Seal exam is Canada's national certification for technicians who diagnose, repair, and maintain passenger vehicles and light-duty trucks. Earning a Red Seal 310S means your expertise is recognized in every Canadian province — a significant advantage in a trade where technician shortages are driving wages to record highs.

The exam consists of 125 multiple-choice questions based on the 2023 Red Seal Occupational Standard (RSOS), with a passing mark of 70%. You have 4 hours to complete the exam.

Topic Breakdown (2023 RSOS)

  • Engine Systems (~22%) — The largest block. Covers engine mechanical (compression, timing, valvetrain), fuel injection systems (GDI, PFI, TFSI), VVT systems, emission controls (EGR, PCV, EVAP), and engine management diagnostics using scan tool data.
  • Electrical & Electronic Systems (~22%) — Equal in weight to engines. CAN bus architecture, multiplexed body systems, BCM/PCM communication, oscilloscope diagnostics, battery and charging systems, parasitic draw testing.
  • Brakes (~18%) — Disc and drum brake service, ABS hydraulic modulator diagnostics, electronic stability control (ESC/VSC), brake fluid serviceability, proportioning valves, and parking brake systems.
  • Suspension & Steering (~18%) — Wheel alignment (camber, caster, toe, SAI), strut/spring replacement, wheel bearing diagnostics, rack-and-pinion service, EPAS systems, and TPMS sensor programming.
  • Drivetrain (~12%) — Automatic transmissions (hydraulic and mechatronic), CVT belt/chain service, AWD/4WD transfer cases, CV axles, and clutch systems for manual transmissions.
  • HVAC & Comfort Systems (~8%) — Refrigerant recovery/recharge procedures, compressor clutch diagnostics, heater core, blend door actuators, and automatic climate control systems.

Exam Strategy for 310S

Electrical is make-or-break. The 310S has shifted heavily toward electrical and electronic diagnostics over the past decade. Candidates who struggle with wiring diagrams, oscilloscope patterns, and CAN bus fault analysis consistently fail this section. Prioritize it.

Use the freeze-frame data. Many diagnostic questions give you a scan tool data PID table or DTC freeze frame. Read all values before answering — the pattern of multiple sensor readings together often reveals the root cause.

Brake questions are straightforward. The brake section is highly rule-based — know FMVSS standards, minimum lining thickness specs, and ABS modulator bleeding procedures cold.
