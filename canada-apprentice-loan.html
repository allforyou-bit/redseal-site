Canada Apprentice Loan: $4,000 per training period, interest-free

Who qualifies, the application window for each period of technical training, how much is left to borrow, and when repayment starts.

Updated November 2026 Checked against the official pages, November 5, 2026
Checked on November 5, 2026 against the Government of Canada’s Canada Apprentice Loan page and the Canada Apprentice Loan Service Centre, where you apply. This page is an independent guide, not a government site and not financial advice. Apply only through the official service.

The short answer

How muchUp to $4,000 per period of technical training, for up to 5 periods, so at most $20,000.
What it can pay forTuition, tools, equipment and living expenses, forgone wages, or supporting your family.
InterestNone. Since April 1, 2023 interest is permanently eliminated on all Canada Apprentice Loans, including loans being repaid. Interest built up before that date is still owed.
Payments while trainingNone while you are confirmed as registered in a Red Seal trade apprenticeship program, for up to 312 weeks (6 years).
Who can applyCitizens, permanent residents and protected persons registered in a designated Red Seal trade apprenticeship program, in block release or equivalent full-time technical training with an approved provider, who pass a credit check the first time.
Not available toHigh school students, apprentices registered in Quebec, and anyone receiving a Canada Student Loan for the same technical training.
When to applyFrom 3 months before a period of technical training starts until its last day.

Check your application window

The window rule (3 months before the start, up to the last day) and the limit of 5 periods are from the Service Centre’s Applying and Basics pages. The checker does not check the other conditions, such as the credit check or the 312-week limit.

Who qualifies

You must meet all of these:

  • be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident or protected person;
  • be registered in a Red Seal trade apprenticeship program that the province or territory where you are registered designates;
  • be enrolled in block release technical training, or the equivalent full-time technical training, with an approved technical training provider; and
  • pass a credit check, if you are applying for the first time.

You are not eligible if you are a high school student, are registered as an apprentice in Quebec, are getting a Canada Student Loan for the same technical training, have been told you are restricted from Canada Apprentice Loans or Canada Student Loans, have already been funded for 5 periods of technical training, or have already used 312 weeks (6 years) of payment-free status.

How to apply

  1. Sign in or register at My Service Canada Account, then choose the Canada Apprentice Loan service.
  2. Apply online, and add your banking details to get the money faster.
  3. Agree to the terms and conditions and submit. You can follow the status in your online account.

You can also mail the paper application and agreement, as long as it and every supporting document are sent by the last day of your technical training.

When repayment starts

Repayment is triggered when any of these happens: you complete your Red Seal apprenticeship program; you stop being registered in one; you reach the lifetime maximum of 312 weeks (6 years) of payment-free status; or your registration is not confirmed by your Apprenticeship End Date. You then have a 6-month non-repayment period before regular payments start, and you can pay earlier if you want. Payments can be made by pre-authorized debit, cheque, online or telephone banking, or money order.

If payments are hard to make, the Repayment Assistance Plan can lower them, or bring them to zero, for 6 months at a time, and you can reapply. A loan with no payments for 270 days (9 months) goes to the Canada Revenue Agency for collection, so contact the Service Centre before that point.

Other support for apprentices

The loan is separate from grants and tax deductions. Journeypersons certified on or after April 1, 2025 can look at the $5,000 Red Seal Completion Bonus, and employed tradespeople who buy their own tools can look at the tradesperson’s tools deduction.

Working on a construction site far from home? The labour mobility deduction covers up to $4,000 a year of temporary lodging and travel for tradespeople and apprentices in construction.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow with the Canada Apprentice Loan?

Up to $4,000 per period of technical training, for up to 5 periods of technical training, so at most $20,000 in all. The money can go toward tuition, tools, equipment and living expenses, forgone wages or supporting your family.

Is the Canada Apprentice Loan interest-free?

Yes. Since April 1, 2023 the Government of Canada has permanently eliminated interest on all Canada Apprentice Loans, including loans already being repaid. Interest that built up on a loan before April 1, 2023 is still owed.

Who qualifies for the Canada Apprentice Loan?

Canadian citizens, permanent residents and protected persons registered in a Red Seal trade apprenticeship program designated by the province or territory where they are registered, enrolled in block release or equivalent full-time technical training with an approved provider, who pass a credit check when applying for the first time. High school students and apprentices registered in Quebec are not eligible.

When can I apply for the Canada Apprentice Loan?

From 3 months before your technical training starts until the last day of that technical training. You apply online through My Service Canada Account, or by mailing the paper application by the last day of training.

When do I have to start repaying the Canada Apprentice Loan?

Repayment is triggered when you complete your Red Seal apprenticeship program, stop being registered in one, reach the lifetime maximum of 312 weeks (6 years) of payment-free status, or your registration is not confirmed by your Apprenticeship End Date. Regular payments start after a further 6-month non-repayment period.

Can I get the Canada Apprentice Loan in Quebec?

No. The loan is available in every province and territory except Quebec, where the Quebec government offers its own student financial assistance.
